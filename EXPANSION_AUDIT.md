# World classics expansion — September 19, 2026

49 works added to the 1,430-entry baseline; 1,479 total entries, all dated and mapped. The batch includes all 12 proposed non-Western candidates, Water Margin, 27 further classics and 9 missing Penguin titles discussed in the preceding audit. This is a curated expansion, not an exhaustive import of every publisher series.

## China’s Four Great Classics

Romance of the Three Kingdoms and Journey to the West retain their existing IDs. Water Margin is new. Dream of the Red Chamber is the same novel as The Story of the Stone: its five existing volume records are renamed and cross-indexed, not duplicated. Thus the four novels occupy eight existing-compatible catalog entries. Collection filters, familiar English titles, transliterations, and Chinese title aliases are searchable.

## Dates, locations, editions

Dates are original composition/publication intervals. Broad estimates are explicitly labeled and linked. Huarochirí and Popol Vuh retain separate later recording dates. Distinct named medieval retellings use their own composition periods. Modern-country associations use author origin and original literary regions; translator nationality is not used. For anonymous texts, these are source-tradition associations, not author biographies. Publisher series labels indicate the source consulted, not exclusive affiliation.

The 31 publisher cover URLs identify product-page images. Selections, omnibus editions and first volumes are labeled. Twelve selected-edition page counts were added; no Goodreads scores were invented. Other missing images remain labeled title art.

## Per-work date decisions

- **A Couple of Soles**: Original work date; modern translation and reprint dates are excluded.
- **A Treatise on Dharma**: Approximate original composition interval, based on the period described in the linked source; not a modern translation date.
- **Arjuna and the Hunter**: Approximate original composition interval, based on the period described in the linked source; not a modern translation date.
- **Breakfast at Tiffany’s**: Original work date; modern translation and reprint dates are excluded.
- **Cantares Mexicanos**: Estimated span of late pre-conquest and colonial songs preserved in a late-sixteenth-century manuscript; individual attributions and earlier origins are uncertain.
- **Changchun’s Journey to the West**: Estimated composition after the 1220–1224 journey, with a 1228 preface; a historical travelogue distinct from the Monkey King novel.
- **Coal**: The 1976 collection includes poems from The First Cities (1968) and Cables to Rage (1970), alongside new poems; earliest composition of every poem is not established.
- **Essays of a Recluse**: Estimated second-century composition during Wang Fu’s adult lifetime (c. 85–162).
- **Florentine Codex**: Sixteenth-century collaborative research and manuscript compilation, completed in 1577; it preserves older knowledge whose individual origins are not securely dated.
- **Giovanni’s Room**: Original work date; modern translation and reprint dates are excluded.
- **Handsome Nanda**: Approximate original composition interval, based on the period described in the linked source; not a modern translation date.
- **Huarochirí Manuscript**: Conservative late pre-conquest to early-colonial tradition window ending with recording around 1608; older roots cannot be dated precisely. Not the 1991 English translation.
- **Jain Tales of Moksha**: Approximate original composition interval, based on the period described in the linked source; not a modern translation date.
- **Kalpa Imperial**: Original work date; modern translation and reprint dates are excluded.
- **Kebra Nagast**: Fourteenth-century Geʽez compilation incorporating older traditions; the era of Solomon is a narrative setting, not a securely dated origin.
- **Kokinshū**: Approximate span of the principal early-Heian poems and compilation, commissioned in 905; some anonymous poems may be older. The linked Columbia edition is a selection, not all 1,100 poems.
- **Life of the Buddha**: Approximate original composition interval, based on the period described in the linked source; not a modern translation date.
- **Lilavai**: Approximate original composition interval, based on the period described in the linked source; not a modern translation date.
- **Nagarakretagama**: Original work date; modern translation and reprint dates are excluded.
- **Night Watch**: Original work date; modern translation and reprint dates are excluded.
- **One Hundred Years of Solitude**: Original work date; modern translation and reprint dates are excluded.
- **Poems from the Satsai**: Approximate original composition interval, based on the period described in the linked source; not a modern translation date.
- **Popol Vuh**: Broad contextual range from Classic Maya attestations of related mythology to the sixteenth-century alphabetic text (c. 1554–1558). This does not date every episode or prove a first telling.
- **Sur’s Ocean**: Approximate original composition interval, based on the period described in the linked source; not a modern translation date.
- **Tarikh al-Sudan**: Chronicle completed around 1655–1656. Sudan in the historical title means the West African region, not today’s country of Sudan; Hunwick’s translation is selective.
- **The Apple in the Dark**: Original work date; modern translation and reprint dates are excluded.
- **The Book of Lord Shang**: Approximate original composition interval, based on the period described in the linked source; not a modern translation date.
- **The Book of Monasteries**: Tenth-century anthology completed by the author’s death in 998/999; some incorporated verse is earlier.
- **The Book of Swindles**: The original collection dates to 1617. The linked More Swindles edition translates a further selection from it, not a new seventeenth-century work.
- **The Diary of 1636**: Estimated writing interval after the 1636–1637 invasion and before the author’s death in 1642; the modern translation is from 2020.
- **The Epic of Ram**: Approximate original composition interval, based on the period described in the linked source; not a modern translation date.
- **The First New Chronicle and Good Government**: Original work date; modern translation and reprint dates are excluded.
- **The Fragrant Companions**: Original work date; modern translation and reprint dates are excluded.
- **The History of Akbar**: Approximate original composition interval, based on the period described in the linked source; not a modern translation date.
- **The Kannada Mahabharata**: Fifteenth-century Kannada work, distinct from the ancient Sanskrit Mahabharata; linked translation begins with Volume 1.
- **The Killing of Shishupala**: Approximate original composition interval, based on the period described in the linked source; not a modern translation date.
- **The Life of Harishchandra**: Approximate original composition interval, based on the period described in the linked source; not a modern translation date.
- **The Life of Padma**: Estimated c. 900 composition following Murty’s dating of Svayambhudeva around the beginning of the tenth century; other chronologies differ. Volume 1 is an edition component, not a separate epic.
- **The Life of Walatta Petros**: The core hagiography was written in 1672; miracles and other later additions are not assigned that date.
- **The Little Clay Cart**: Approximate original composition interval, based on the period described in the linked source; not a modern translation date.
- **The Original Meaning of the Yijing**: Approximate original composition interval, based on the period described in the linked source; not a modern translation date.
- **The Pursuit of Love**: Original novel published in 1945. The linked modern Penguin edition is an omnibus also containing Love in a Cold Climate and The Blessing; its 512 pages are not assigned to this single novel.
- **The Rules of Logic**: Approximate original composition interval, based on the period described in the linked source; not a modern translation date.
- **The Story of Manu**: Approximate original composition interval, based on the period described in the linked source; not a modern translation date.
- **The Turks and the Caliphal Army**: Estimated composition in the Abbasid context after al-Muʿtasim’s accession in 833 and before al-Jahiz’s death in 869.
- **Theft of a Tree**: Approximate original composition interval, based on the period described in the linked source; not a modern translation date.
- **Top Graduate Zhang Xie**: Wide disputed composition range: often assigned to the thirteenth century, surviving in an early-fifteenth-century textual witness; Columbia explicitly leaves earlier composition open.
- **Trafalgar**: Original work date; modern translation and reprint dates are excluded.
- **Water Margin**: Estimated formative late-fourteenth-century novel; authorship is disputed and the text evolved into the sixteenth century. The twelfth-century setting is not its composition date.
