# Story-origin date review — 2026-09-19

The timeline prioritizes the recoverable origin/formation of traditional narratives over their modern collection, performance, translation or adaptation date. Fourteen entries receive reviewed dates and separate version metadata. All changes are estimated, with contextual inferences clearly identified. Dates of legendary events are not automatically treated as dates of composition.

## Scope and limitations

The title-based review isolated 74 entries containing tale, myth, folklore, legend, saga, epic, fairy or fable, plus relevant named retellings and oral-tradition notes. Original authored fiction is not automatically backdated because it uses folklore. Already ancient/medieval composition ranges are generally retained. This is a focused origin-date audit, not a claim to have established the first telling of every story.

African Myths of Origin contains many traditions of different ages. Its ancient-to-early-modern envelope is a browsing aid, not a date shared by all 71 chapters. The details separate five source-tradition groups; individual origins not established by evidence are explicitly marked uncertain. These notes are a partial breakdown, not a complete item-level index. The map and filters continue counting each catalog volume once.

Manas has disputed origins. The 1400–1800 window is a tentative late-medieval/early-modern estimate; it must not be described as a proven medieval composition date. Older origin claims are mentioned in the note but not adopted as certain.

## The Memorial Feast for Kökötöy Khan

Tentative late-medieval to early-modern formation window for the Manas story tradition. The tradition's age is disputed: claims of much earlier origins coexist with arguments for substantial sixteenth–eighteenth-century formation. These rounded bounds are an editorial browsing estimate, not established dates for this episode's first telling. Orozbak uulu's 1920s dictation and the 2022 English edition are later witnesses, not the oral story's origin.

Later version reference: Orozbak uulu's recorded performance — 1922 AD – 1926 AD.

c. 1400 AD – 1800 AD. Broad, disputed formation estimate. Individual motifs may be older; no exact medieval composition date is known.

## The Book of Dede Korkut

Broad origin-and-development range for the Oghuz oral stories, with early layers commonly connected to the ninth–tenth centuries and recognisable versions developed by the thirteenth–fourteenth centuries. Scholars differ on these layers. Fifteenth–sixteenth-century manuscripts are later witnesses, not the beginning of the entire tradition.

Later version reference: Written compilation / surviving manuscript tradition — 1400 AD – 1600 AD.

- [Reference 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Book_of_Dede_Korkut)
