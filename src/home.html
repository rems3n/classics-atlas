What you can do

Everything below works without an account, except sharing collections.

Browse by place

See which books come from a country or region.

Click a country on the map. Switch to historical borders, from 1500 BC to AD 1600, in the Time & borders panel.

Browse by period

Show only books written in a date range.

Drag across the timeline, or pick an era such as Ancient or Medieval.

Search and filter

Find a title or author, or narrow by form, tradition and language.

Type a title, an author, or a phrase like “Japanese novels” or “Greek poetry before 400 BC”.

Follow a reading path

Work through a list based on a course or expert selection, one assignment at a time.

Open Paths. Each path shows its source, what it covers, and which items are excerpts or abridgments.

Track your reading

Mark books as want to read, reading or read. Add ratings, notes and favourites.

Open any book and use the controls under its title. My Atlas shows your reading on the map.

Make collections

Group books into your own lists with notes, and share them by link.

Open a book and choose Add to collection. Sharing a collection needs an account.

Reading paths

Paths are based on public course pages and published expert lists. They are not complete degree syllabuses. Each path says what it includes and what it leaves out.

Without an account

About the data

  • The catalog starts from the Wikipedia list of Penguin Classics and adds curated world literature. It is not the full current Penguin catalog.
  • Dates are when a work was first written or published, not the date of a modern edition. Estimated dates are marked “c.” and explained in each book’s notes.
  • Map locations use the author’s birthplace where known, otherwise the work’s literary tradition. Each book says which one applies.
  • Covers come from publishers and Open Library. No ratings are invented.
